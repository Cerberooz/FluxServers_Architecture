Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Flux Servers, we collect minimal data to provide our services:

  • Account Info: Name and Email address provided during ticket creation or ordering.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses and browser information for security and DDoS mitigation.
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies (like _fxt) to maintain your session and ticket access.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely for:

  • Providing technical support via our ticket system.
  • Processing service orders and provisioning.
  • Protecting our network from abuse and attacks.
  • Improving site performance and user experience.

3. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure database management, to protect your data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers for specific functions:

  • Cloudflare: For DDoS protection and global content delivery.
  • Payment Processors: To securely handle financial transactions.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your data or a copy of the information we hold about you by contacting our support team.
